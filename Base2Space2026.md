# Base 2 Space 2026 

## Climbing all 832 stairs at the Seattle Space Needle to raise money for the Fred Hutch Cancer Research through the Space Needle Foundation

I am participating in Seattle’s Most Iconic Climb, Base 2 Space to support Fred Hutch Cancer Research through the Space Needle Foundation. Please consider donating and help us get closer to our $1,000,0000 goal! 
I lost my father when he was 58 and I lost my mother-in-law when she was 59 years old, both to cancer. I have been on this earth longer than my father and mother-in-law.
Please help me surpass my fundraising goal for Seattle’s Most Iconic Climb, Base 2 Space to support Fred Hutch Cancer Research through the Space Needle Foundation. Thank to everyone who has donated so far, I really appreciate it.
